## 1. Educational Tool Notice
GradCast is an academic transcript analysis and degree progress audit engine. It provides informational graduation projections and does not replace official university registrar records.

## 2. Privacy Defaults & Local-First Processing
By default, academic records and transcripts are evaluated locally with zero remote data egress.

## 3. Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)
A complete, machine-readable CycloneDX Software Bill of Materials (`bom.json`) is generated on build in CI via OWASP `@cyclonedx/cdxgen` and attached to release artifacts.
